Install it on your Quest

Everything happens in the headset. No computer needed.

  1. Open this page in the Meta Quest Browser.

  2. Select Download Bonk Slop — Quest APK to download BonkSlop.apk.

  3. Open the headset's Files app and find BonkSlop.apk under Downloads.

  4. Select the file's ⋯ menu, then Open With → Package Installer.

  5. Choose Install, or Update if you already have Bonk Slop.

  6. Open your Library, switch the filter to Unknown Sources, and launch Bonk Slop.

  7. In the cabin, use the lobby computer and enter a room code to join your friends.

No “Package Installer” in Open With?

Your headset needs Developer Mode to install games from outside the Meta Horizon Store. You turn it on from your phone, once:

  1. If you haven't yet, create a free developer organization at developers.meta.com (Meta may ask you to verify your account).
  2. In the Meta Horizon app on your phone, go to Devices → your headset → Headset settings → Developer Mode and switch it on.
  3. Restart the headset, then try step 3 again.

Play with friends: room codes

In the cabin there's a beige lobby computer with a green screen. Press its big keys with your fingertip.

BONK SLOP LOBBY
ROOM: PEAK
IN ROOM PEAK · 1/5 PLAYERS

  1. Wait until the screen says SERVERS WORK NOW. The game then joins a public server by itself (the screen says joining a public server...). The steps below are for a private room with your friends.

  2. One person makes the room: type a code of 4 to 6 letters or numbers (like P E A K) and press CREATE. Then tell your friends the code.

  3. Everyone else joins: type the same code and press JOIN.

  4. Up to 5 players per room. DEL removes a letter, and LEAVE takes you back to playing on your own.

  • NO ROOM CALLED … Check the code, or ask the host to press CREATE first.
  • Private server is full. Try a public server. That room has 5 players: press JOIN PUBLIC SERVER on the computer, or pick a new code.
  • Private rooms aren't listed anywhere, and the automatic public join never puts you in one: only people who know the code can join.
  • Everyone should have the same version (v2.17). Servers are in the US West region, so friends far away can join but may lag more.

Updating later

Download the new version the same way and choose Update. Don't uninstall first: your coins, gold, unlocks and items are saved on the headset, and uninstalling deletes them.

Progress stays on your headset. It isn't linked to an account and doesn't move between headsets.

Privacy policy

Bonk Slop privacy policy · last updated October 22, 2026. Bonk Slop is a VR game made by Dummonk1403 (team Amazing Gorilla). This covers the game on the Meta Horizon Store and the version on this page.

What the game keeps about you

  • Your game account: an account ID our server makes for you, the display name you pick (in the store version it can start as your Meta username, if Meta shares it), your role (player or staff), the game version you use and when you were last online. A secret that proves the account is yours stays on your headset; the server only keeps a scrambled (hashed) copy of it.
  • Online play: while you are in a room, your gorilla’s position and movement, your display name and what you do in the game are shared with the other players in that room through Photon (Exit Games) multiplayer servers. Our server also notes which room you are in while you are online, so staff can keep rooms safe.
  • Safety and moderation: reports you send or that are sent about you (the reason, an optional note and the room), bans, and a log of what staff did.
  • Microphone: only used to move your gorilla’s mouth when you talk. The headset measures how loud you are and shares that single loudness level with your room. Your voice is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere, and you can turn the microphone off in the game.
  • Your progress (coins, items, unlocks) is saved on your headset.
  • Your internet address (IP) is used for a short time only to stop people from flooding the server with requests. It is not used to identify you.

Meta: the store version uses the Meta Platform to check that your Meta account owns the game and, if you allow it, to read your Meta username. Meta’s own privacy policy covers that.

What we never do: no ads, no selling or sharing your data for marketing, no third-party tracking or analytics.

Where it is kept: the account server runs on Cloudflare; multiplayer runs on Photon. Both may store data outside your country.

Kids: Bonk Slop is meant for players 13 and older.

Deleting your data: ask us and we will delete your account and its data within 30 days (we may keep a ban record if it is needed to keep other players safe). Deleting the game from your headset deletes your saved progress.

Contact: open an issue at github.com/Dummonk1403/bonk-slop/issues. Changes to this policy are posted on this page.
